#### FNV-2026-10-12-D2-01: INFO `DATA` Flags 2 — Say Once a Day and Always Darken — is decoded into `InfoDataHeader::flags2` and never read, so 105 FalloutNV.esm and 89 DLC INFOs repeat on every conversation
- **Severity**: LOW. A fidelity gap on barks and greetings; nothing crashes or corrupts state.
- **Dimension**: ESM Data Slice -> dialogue runtime (`/audit-gameplay` Dim 2 owns the mechanism).
- **Location**:
  - `crates/plugin/src/esm/records/misc/dialogue.rs:396-398` (the `flags2` field) and `:725` (the decode).
  - The consumers of `InfoRecord::say_once()` / `goodbye()` / `random()` / `random_end()` in `crates/scripting/src/dialogue.rs:340` read `Flags 1` only.
- **Status**: NEW. No open issue, closed issue or audit mentions Say Once a Day. A grep for `once_a_day`, `fSayOncePerDay` and `Always Darken` finds only the field's doc comment.
- **Description**:
  - FO3/FNV INFO `DATA` byte 3 is `Flags 2`: bit 0 = Say Once a Day, bit 1 = Always Darken (xEdit `wbINFO`; the field's own doc names both).
  - The GECK wiki (`Category/Dialogue`) defines Say Once a Day as "this info will only be played once per 'day' per actor", with the day length set by the `fSayOncePerDayInfoTimer` game setting (12 hours).
  - The decoder stores the byte, but no reader consumes it. `select_info` therefore treats such an INFO as repeatable on every conversation.
  - Always Darken is a prompt-shading hint and has no selection effect.
- **Evidence**: Probe `sayday.py` over the shipped masters, counting INFOs whose `DATA[3] & 1` is set:

  | Plugin | INFOs | Say Once a Day |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | FalloutNV.esm | 23,247 | 105 |
  | DeadMoney.esm | 2,151 | 16 |
  | HonestHearts.esm | 1,238 | 1 |
  | OldWorldBlues.esm | 1,620 | 71 |
  | LonesomeRoad.esm | 667 | 1 |

  - By topic: `IdleChatter` 62 lines, `GREETING` 8, plus DLC radio, sink and brain barks.
  - `Always Darken` (bit 1) is set on 6 FalloutNV.esm INFOs.
- **Impact**: A Say-Once-a-Day greeting or bark that should yield to the next qualifying INFO after its first play stays first in file order and is spoken on every conversation. This is the "welcome to my shop" repeat the flag exists to prevent. Small: roughly 0.45% of FNV INFOs, mostly idle chatter.
- **Related**: #5300 (Say Once ledger), #5367 (dialogue layers), `docs/engine/dialogue-trees.md`.
- **Suggested Fix**: Either extend the spoken ledger with a game-time stamp so a Say-Once-a-Day INFO is skipped for `fSayOncePerDayInfoTimer` hours per `(speaker, INFO)`, or document the flag as unmodelled in `dialogue-trees.md` next to the other unmodelled INFO flags.
